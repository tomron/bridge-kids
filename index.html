Welcome to Bridge!

Bridge is a card game for 4 players. You sit at the bottom and play against the computer who controls the other 3 seats.

Your goal: win tricks (rounds of cards) and make your contract!

The Cards

There are 4 suits: ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣. The trump suit (shown in gold) beats all other suits!

When a suit is led, you must follow suit if you can. If you can't, you may play a trump to win!

Bidding

Before playing, you bid how many tricks you think you'll win, plus which suit is trump.

A bid of 2♥ means "I'll win at least 8 tricks (6 + 2) with hearts as trump." The highest bid becomes the contract!

Winning Tricks

Each player plays one card. The highest card of the led suit wins — unless someone plays a trump card, which beats everything!

Win enough tricks to make your contract and you score points. Fall short and the computer scores instead!
